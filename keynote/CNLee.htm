Title: Chaotic Coefficient Scrambling and Syntax Element Encryption for H.265/HEVC

Abstract:Existing selective encryption schemes for H.265/HEVC video often suffer from high computational complexity, increased bit rate overhead, and limited security. To address these issues, in this talk it will first give an introduction on H.265/HEVC selective encryption. Then present a chaotic map-based coefficient scrambling scheme integrated with syntax element encryption (SEC). By exploiting the pseudorandomness and sensitivity to initial conditions of chaotic maps, the proposed scheme enhances encryption security while minimizing computational and bit rate overhead. SEC further protects video content by encrypting motion vector differences (MVDs), quantized transform coefficients (QTCs), and luma intra-prediction modes (Luma IPMs). Slice-level synchronization enables independent decryption of unaffected slices even when some slices are lost. Additionally, AES-CTR-based pseudorandom sequences are incorporated to strengthen resistance against chosen-plaintext attacks. Experimental results will be given to demonstrate the performance of the proposed scheme.
