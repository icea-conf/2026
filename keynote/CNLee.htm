Keynote Speech Information - Lee, Chung-Nan


  1. Professor at National Sun Yat-sen University
Biography: Chung-Nan Lee Professor Lee Tsung-nan is a Distinguished Professor and former Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at National Sun Yat-sen University (now a retired professor). He holds a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Washington. He is highly respected not only in academia and the field of AI, but also became an internet sensation after a video of him single-handedly taking on a Formosan macaque with a broom went viral, earning him the nickname "Lu Bu of Sun Yat-sen" from students and netizens.
